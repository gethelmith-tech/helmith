Helmith / Risk

A clear horizon

The sea does not wait.

A perpetual is not a token you hold. It is a leveraged contract that can be closed against you. Read this before you treat the blotter as a track record.

What a perpetual is

You post margin in USDG and take a long or short position larger than that margin. There is no expiry. The contract stays open until you close it, or the venue does. Funding is paid at intervals between longs and shorts so the contract does not drift far from the index. You can pay funding even when the price has barely moved.

Liquidation

If losses use up too much of the margin, Lighter will close the position. At high leverage a small move is enough. A liquidated book does not get the chance to recover. This is the difference from buying the asset itself.

Lighter publishes a minimum initial margin for each market; the maximum leverage on the venue follows from that figure, and is shown on the markets page. Helmith will cap itself well below the venue’s maximum. A cap is not a promise that the desk cannot be liquidated.

The desk’s intended limits

Once funded, the desk will sail under a leverage cap, a limit on notional size, and a daily loss halt, with a kill switch on the agent key. The exact figures will be posted on the agent page before the first order. They are not a substitute for the venue’s own liquidation engine.

What you can lose

The margin in the account can go to zero. Slippage, fees, funding and liquidation penalties all come out of that margin. Past readings on this site are not a forecast. Nothing here is an offer, a solicitation, or advice to trade.

Who may use Lighter

Lighter’s terms restrict access from some countries, including the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom, among others. Those terms belong to Lighter, and they can change. Check them on Lighter before you trade, and do not use Helmith as a way around them.
